# Sonalii Portfolio: Section-by-Section Colour Refresh

The landing page gets a new colour scheme. Each of the 8 sections gets its own shade, tint or tone from the pink and red family, with chocolate brown as the anchor.
Light and deep sections take turns down the page, so it has clear rhythm and red is visible throughout. Pink is no longer almost the only colour. Layouts and content stay as they are; only colours change.

## Who it's for
- **Brands and agencies** scrolling the page on a phone. The changing colours mark where each section starts and keep the page memorable.
- **Sonalii**, who wants the page to feel bolder and more "her" while staying minimal and elegant.

## Current problem (after reviewing every section)
- 6 of the 8 sections use near-identical pale pinks: Hero, About, Hire Me, Work, Kind Words and Approach. Scrolling feels like one long pink page.
- Red only appears in the Collaborations panel, the Approach quote box and small accents (buttons, hearts).
- Section edges are hard to see because neighbouring backgrounds are almost the same colour.

## Core features and experience: new colour per section

| # | Section | Light / deep | New background | Colour family | What else changes |
|---|---------|--------------|----------------|---------------|-------------------|
| 1 | Hero | Light | **Ballet pink** (soft, slightly cooler pink) | Pink tint | Headline in chocolate. The "View my work" button fills with wine red on hover |
| 2 | About | Mid | **Dusty rose** (muted, greyed pink) | Pink tone | The white polaroid stands out more. The tape becomes pearl pink. Labels and the heart turn wine red |
| 3 | Collaborations | Deep + light split | Left panel **lipstick red**, right panel **pearl pink** (very pale) | Pure red + palest pink tint | Same split layout as now. Brand names in chocolate turn lipstick red on hover. Hearts in red |
| 4 | Hire Me | Light | **Rose quartz** (cool mauve-pink) | Pink tint | The form sits on a pearl-pink card so it's easy to read. Collab chips fill red on hover. The submit button is lipstick red |
| 5 | Selected Work | Deep | **Wine / burgundy** (dark red) | Red shade | Works like a gallery wall, so photos and reel tiles stand out. Labels and text are in pale pink. Placeholder tiles are in dusty rose |
| 6 | Kind Words | Light | **Strawberry pink** (warm, rosy red tint) | Red tint | Big quote marks in wine red. Quotes in chocolate. Dividers in deeper rose |
| 7 | Approach | Deep | **Raspberry red** (rich mid-dark red) | Red shade | Step numbers in pale pink, titles and text in pearl pink. The closing quote box flips to ballet pink with red text |
| 8 | Footer | Deep | **Chocolate brown** (unchanged) | Brown | Accent words and the email button in strawberry pink to tie back to the page |

**Overall rhythm:** Ballet pink → Dusty rose → **Red** / pearl → Rose quartz → **Wine** → Strawberry → **Raspberry** → **Chocolate**.
That gives three different reds (lipstick, wine, raspberry), five different pinks, and brown to close the page.

**Small details that follow each section's colour:**
- Section labels (e.g. "COLLABORATIONS") switch colour for contrast: wine on light sections, pale pink on deep ones.
- Hearts, divider lines and hover colours match each section's palette.
- The top bar stays a soft blurred pearl pink, so it reads clearly over both light and deep sections.
- Text always keeps strong contrast: chocolate on light backgrounds, pearl pink on deep ones.

## User flow
Same as today. Visitors scroll through Hero → About → Collaborations → Hire Me → Selected Work → Kind Words → Approach → Footer, or jump there from the top bar. Each colour change marks the start of a new section.

## UI/UX feel
- Still minimal and editorial: the same serif headlines, wide-spaced labels and calm animations.
- Bolder and warmer: deep red sections give the page confidence, and the pink sections in between keep it soft and feminine.
- Every section should be identifiable by its colour alone, even in a quick scroll.
- On phones the alternating colours also act as clear section dividers.

## Implementation phases
**Phase 1 (built now)**
- Recolour all 8 sections exactly as in the table above, including labels, hearts, dividers, buttons and hover states.
- Check readability on every background, on both desktop and phone.

**Phase 2**
- Fine-tune any single shade after Sonalii sees it live, e.g. a lighter wine or a softer strawberry.
- Once real photos and reels are added, adjust the Selected Work wine shade so they look their best on it.

**Phase 3**
- Optional seasonal or campaign palettes (e.g. a festive red theme) that can be switched on later.

## Assumptions
- Hero and About get new shades, as chosen. The Hero is no longer the exact mockup pink, but its layout matches the mockup as before.
- The Collaborations split layout stays. Only its two colours are refreshed (lipstick red + pearl pink).
- Light and deep sections take turns, as chosen. The last two sections (raspberry Approach and chocolate Footer) are both deep. This is intentional, so the page ends on a strong note.
- Shade names are descriptive. Exact colour values may shift slightly during the build for readability, but each stays in its stated family.
- No content, layout, form behaviour or email behaviour changes.
- The hero photo's own pink backdrop inside the oval stays as it is.
